Why we stayed small

Smaller on purpose

Plenty of operators will put you on a coach with forty other people and call it an adventure. We went the other way.

Four guests, maximum

We specialise in customised, personal adventures, so we keep the groups very small — usually a maximum of four people. Larger group? Tell us. Some activities can take more.

One price, everything in it

Guide, fuel, national park and camping permits, food and drink on the adventure, and accommodation if you need it. You are not handed a bill of extras at the end of the week.

The person who answers your email comes with you

No call centre and no subcontracted driverplaceholder — proposed positioning, not a description of how Still Wild is staffed today. You plan the trip with the same crew who will be standing next to you when you get there.

Money

One price, and here is exactly what is in it

Prices vary with the adventure, what you want to see and the route you take. What does not vary is the list.

The fee includes

  • Guide fee — your guide, for the whole adventure
  • Fuel
  • National park permits
  • Camping permits, where they are available
  • Food and drink during the adventure
  • Accommodation if it is needed

Not included

  • Accommodation before and after the adventure, unless you ask for it
  • Airfares to Australia
  • Food and drink outside the adventure
  • Personal spending money
  • Your private travel and health insurance — required, see below
No prices on this page — on purpose. Every adventure is custom. Send us your adventure wishes and we will build a customised adventure and email you the pricing options.

Safety

Yes, we know this is the boring part

We would rather put it on the front page than bury it in the small print. Some adventures do contain elements of danger, and we take every precaution and care to manage it.

Before any Still Wild adventure you will

  1. Take part in a safety briefing covering the specific adventure you have chosen.
  2. Complete safety training if it is needed — rock climbing, mountaineering, fire building, swimming, boating, canoeing and similar activities each have their own skills and rules.
  3. Agree to follow the guides' instructions at all times while on the adventure.
  4. Show proof of a valid, current insurance policy.

Travel and health insurance is compulsory

Australian government regulations mean that to come on a Still Wild adventure you must present a valid and current certificate, or receipt of payment, for your own private travel and health insurance.

Even the great explorers of the past had cover for the unfortunate. Get it sorted before you fly and you can put your whole mind into being an adventurer instead of worrying.

Before you write

How many people can come?
We specialise in customised, personal adventures, so groups stay very small and personal — usually a maximum of four. If you have a larger group, tell us anyway; some activities can take more.
How long should we come for?
For a normal adventure we recommend three to five days. For a survival adventure, seven days is the minimum — anything shorter and you spend the whole trip getting used to it.
What will it cost?
Prices vary with each individual adventure: what you want to see and do, and the route you take. Send us your adventure wishes and we will build the trip and email you the pricing options.
Do I really need my own insurance?
Yes. Australian government regulations mean you must present a valid, current certificate or receipt of payment for your own private travel and health insurance before you can join a Still Wild adventure.
你们说中文吗? Do you speak Chinese?
Yes — the Still Wild site is published in English and 中文, and you can reach us on WeChat at Still_Wild2015. The Chinese version of this concept page is not built yet.

Brisbane, usually. From there we can be on a 4WD track, a beach or a national park within a couple of hours, and anywhere in Queensland within a day.placeholder
